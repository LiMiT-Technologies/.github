## About LiMiT

**LiMiT** is a technology-driven company building and operating proprietary digital systems across software, infrastructure, automation, AI-assisted workflows, data-centric online businesses, and capital-backed digital platforms.

Founded in Romania and active since **2007**, LiMiT evolved from web development and high-performance digital execution into a broader technology ecosystem focused on resilient systems, proprietary tools, automation layers, market intelligence, and long-term digital asset growth.

LiMiT is not built as a conventional agency.

It operates more like a compact technical holding: designing systems, deploying infrastructure, owning platforms, improving digital assets, automating workflows, and using software as leverage across multiple online verticals.
